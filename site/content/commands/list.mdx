---
title: desk list
description: Show every workspace with its state, retention reason, and location.
---

## Usage

```text title="Usage"
desk list
```

`list` takes no arguments or flags.

## Output

```text
ID            NAME          STATE        RETENTION                                         PATH
bb5ff6487d99  "experiment"  ready        eligible with --quiescent                         /home/you/.desk/workspaces/bb5ff6487d99
702378b08f9e  "scratch"     quarantined  quarantine age: seven days required before purge  /home/you/.desk/quarantine/702378b08f9e
bf22fab17609  "notes"       done         pinned                                            /home/you/.desk/workspaces/bf22fab17609
```

## Columns

<ParamField name="ID">
The workspace ID used by every other command.
</ParamField>
<ParamField name="NAME">
The label, quoted.
</ParamField>
<ParamField name="STATE">
`ready`, `done`, or `quarantined`. See [Workspaces](/docs/workspaces/#states).
</ParamField>
<ParamField name="RETENTION">
The first [retention rule](/docs/retention-rules/) that keeps the workspace, or what it is eligible for:

- `eligible with --quiescent`: it could be quarantined now;
- `quarantine age: seven days required before purge`: it is quarantined and too young to purge;
- `eligible for purge with --quiescent`: it is quarantined and could be purged.
</ParamField>
<ParamField name="PATH">
The directory's actual location, in storage or in quarantine.
</ParamField>

<Callout type="tip">
`desk list` runs the full retention checks, including `git ls-remote` for repository workspaces, so it may take a moment when you have many repositories.
</Callout>
