---
title: desk keep
description: Pin a workspace so cleanup never moves it, or unpin it.
---

## Usage

```text title="Usage"
desk keep ID [--off]
```

## Arguments

<ParamField name="ID" type="workspace ID" required>
The workspace to pin or unpin.
</ParamField>

## Flags

<ParamField name="--off" type="flag">
Remove the pin instead of adding it.
</ParamField>

## Behavior

A pinned workspace is retained by `gc`, `remove`, and `purge`, whatever its contents. `desk done` also refuses pinned workspaces.

Pins are kept when a workspace is quarantined and restored.

## Examples

```sh
desk keep bf22fab17609         # pin
desk keep bf22fab17609 --off   # unpin
```
