---
title: desk rm
description: Move a workspace to garbage after a summary and confirmation.
---

## Usage

```text title="Usage"
desk rm [NAME]
```

## Arguments

<ParamField name="NAME" type="optional">
A workspace name or a unique prefix of one. Leave it out to delete the workspace you're in.
</ParamField>

## Behavior

desk summarizes the workspace, then asks before moving it to garbage:

- a scratch workspace shows whether it is `empty` or `has files`;
- a repository shows its origin, the number of uncommitted changes, and the number of commits on local branches that no remote-tracking branch contains.

Only `y` or `yes` removes it. Anything else, including an empty answer, keeps the workspace and exits with status `1`. With [shell integration](/docs/shell-integration/), if you removed the workspace you were in, your shell moves to your home directory.

Removed workspaces stay in `~/.desk/garbage` for 72 hours from removal (`DESK_HOME` overrides the root). Every desk invocation silently collects expired garbage; there is no daemon.

## Example

```sh
desk rm
```

```text
Remove misty-ember (https://github.com/octocat/Hello-World.git; 2 uncommitted changes, 1 unpushed commits)? Retain in garbage for 72 hours. [y/N] y
desk: moved misty-ember to garbage (retained for 72 hours)
```

<Callout type="warning">
The summary is information, not a safety check: explicit removal moves whatever you confirm, including local-only work. After the 72-hour retention period, garbage can be permanently deleted by any desk invocation. Use [`desk cleanup`](/commands/cleanup/) for eligibility-checked removal instead. Unpushed commits are counted against your last fetch.
</Callout>
